A browser recreation of a PyBullet project: a ResNet-18 classifies each 3D object from a rendered crop (angled at −45°, matching training), and a 7-DOF KUKA-style arm sorts it into the labeled bin — the model's prediction is the only source of identity. The detector panel mirrors the live classification; the real model and physics run in run_robot.py.
